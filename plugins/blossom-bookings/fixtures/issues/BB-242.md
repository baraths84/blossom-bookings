# BB-242 — Coupon management and booking redemption

Build local promotion management at /coupons: list, create, edit, activate, deactivate and delete. Codes are unique after trimming and uppercasing; accept 1–32 letters, numbers, underscores and hyphens. Description is at most 200 characters. Percentage is an integer from 1 through 100.

Checkout accepts one active code case-insensitively, supports replace/remove, and reports unknown/inactive codes without applying a discount. Compute the percentage of nightly subtotal, round to nearest cent, and show subtotal, savings and final total. Revalidate the current coupon at booking creation and persist code, subtotal, discount and total. Confirmation must retain savings after refresh. Do not trust a caller-provided total. All validation is local to this fictional demo; a production reservation system would need a trusted backend.

Test management, duplicate codes, invalid percentages, lowercase redemption, fractional-cent rounding, replacement/removal, date changes, inactive codes at confirmation and persisted savings. No taxes, fees, real payments or authentication.

## Resolved requirements
Use automatic recalculation when dates change and the shared blue light/dark theme. Invalidated coupons receive no discount and block confirmation until removed or replaced.
